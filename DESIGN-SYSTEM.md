# Website design system

The current user direction governs the existing visual layers.

- Brand accent and browser theme: `#358AD2`; dark surfaces: `#071E32`; white and pale blue supporting surfaces.
- Primary buttons and active menu items use bright brand blue `#247AB5` with white text. The hover color is `#1B6499`. Hero secondary actions use a clear white outline.
- Typeface: self-hosted Poppins, weights 400, 500, 600, and 700, with Segoe UI and sans-serif fallbacks. The font license is in `assets/fonts/Poppins-OFL.txt`. No visitor-side remote font request is required.
- Body, navigation, labels, form fields, buttons, and ordinary captions: 16px minimum. Reading paragraphs can be 18px. The hero chapter label is 12px; counters and resource metadata can be 14px.
- Homepage headline: fluid 36–60px, reduced further on narrow or short viewports. Supporting content stays centered.
- Background sections span the viewport. Content containers have a 1528px maximum with responsive gutters. The homepage video is an inset card with 12px white gutters (8px on mobile) and rounded corners. Card and gutters together occupy 100vh.
- Initial navigation: white pill over the video or photograph. Scrolled navigation: transparent links on a navy header flush with the top edge.
- Hero: existing five-chapter footage, no decorative circle, a light full-frame scrim plus feathered contrast behind the copy and soft text shadows, a progress ring around the play/pause button, and unboxed previous/next/pause controls.
- SVG icons retain accessible labels on their parent controls. Focus rings and keyboard navigation remain available. Reduced-motion preferences suppress automatic hero playback and entrance movement.
- Shared page sections use editorial photographs, connected frameworks, process steps, outcome panels, and narrative next-chapter links.

- The entire website, through the footer, sits inside one white card with a 5px border and rounded outer corners. The surrounding body is soft blue-gray `#E6EBEF`. Desktop outer gutter: 12px; mobile: 8px with a 4px white border.
