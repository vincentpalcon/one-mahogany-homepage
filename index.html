A useful place to start

Look at the Work.
Find the Friction.

You don't need a complicated assessment. Start with one task that keeps coming back to you.

  1. Follow one customer inquiry.

    Where does it arrive? Who responds? Where is the next step recorded? Look for the point where an inquiry can get lost.

  2. Notice the work only you can do.

    Which decisions need your experience? Which repeated tasks come back to you because nobody else has clear ownership?

  3. Look at one handoff.

    When work moves between people, is it clear who owns the next step, what they need, and when it should happen?

You can bring the situation before you know the solution.
